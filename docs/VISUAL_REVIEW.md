# Design concepts versus working product

The three original AI-generated images are design targets, not screenshots. The actual product captures are generated by Playwright from the release build at a fixed desktop viewport; they are not AI renderings. The `browser-review` artifact on CI includes current individual screens and labeled comparison boards. The committed images below preserve the v0.2 release; v0.2.2 captures are produced by the updated CI suite.

| Screen | Intent retained | Current implementation | Remaining visual/product work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Dark navy, lime actions, persistent navigation, Relay campaign | Daily practice plan, mission continuation, counts, recommendations and service map | Richer campaign path and isometric illustration |
| Mission | Briefing, workspace, hints, evidence | Staged practice, workspace tabs, incident notebook, executable Python, optional Bash and handoff | Full Linux VM lab lifecycle and richer incident environments |
| Design Lab | Change conditions, inject failure, explain tradeoffs | Editable topology, finite queues, baselines, failure scenarios and saved rationale | Richer component library, calibrated models and practitioner-reviewed scenarios |
| Review | Evidence-based learning | Concept schedule, independent practice and transfer variants | Practitioner-calibrated scheduling and longitudinal learning feedback |
| Account | Same navigation, dark panels and lime actions | Login, registration, save status, guest import, export and conflict controls | Public-service identity and self-service recovery |
| Author | Extensible original curriculum | Validated JSON editor, lesson preview, import/export | Structured form editor and editorial collaboration |

The standalone captures show the tutor disconnected. The original concepts show an optional tutor-connected state. Review screenshots use test-fixture progress; Account screenshots use a disposable test account and that fixture progress, not a real learner's activity.
